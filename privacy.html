Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Compositor handles information when the account owner uses the service to automate parts of a short-form content workflow.

Information Compositor may access

When the account owner authorises TikTok, Compositor may access information covered by the specific TikTok permissions granted, such as basic account information, profile information, account statistics, the owner's video list, and permissions required to upload or publish content. Compositor does not request access beyond the permissions needed for the enabled workflow.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the account owner, prepare and manage content, display relevant account or content information, upload or publish content requested by the account owner, and verify publishing status.

AI-generated content and disclosures

If content is generated or materially edited with AI, the workflow is designed to preserve required platform disclosures and to let the account owner make required publishing choices before content is posted.

Storage and service providers

The workflow may use authorised third-party infrastructure and automation services, including Composio, GitHub, Dropbox, Todoist, and other services selected by the account owner. Data is shared with those services only as needed to operate the workflow under their respective terms and privacy policies. Compositor does not sell TikTok user data.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as needed to operate the workflow or as stored by the account owner in connected services. The account owner can remove stored workflow files from those services and revoke TikTok access through TikTok's account or security settings.

Security

Authorisation is performed using provider-supported authentication where available. Access credentials should not be placed in public repositories or shared publicly.

Your choices

The account owner may revoke TikTok access at any time. Revoking access stops Compositor from using the revoked TikTok permissions until authorisation is granted again.

Children

Compositor is not intended for use by children and is designed as a personal workflow for an adult account owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the workflow, permissions, or service providers change. The current version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the owner through the project owner's GitHub profile.
